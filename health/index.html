Purpose

We started back in 2019 when members of our team were collaborating with fitness and nutrition gurus in these fields of expertise to bring forth "Health & Wellbeing". Our brand emerged in the early 2000's through trial and error to help address common concerns that many people had in our local community and online platforms. We continued our research and found our niche to bring to you our passion of nutrition and fitness online. Our chosen platform of online branding has allowed us to reach out to those in need outside of the Hamilton, New Zealand region. We look forward to working with you in assisting you in your own nutrition and/or fitness goals for your own health and wellbeing journey.

Meet the Team

Meet Laura, Ashley, Demi and Michelle! The start up team of our whole brand. A group of mums who met at their local playgroup and all had a passion to improve their health and wellbeing. Laura is a profound yoga and pilates instructor with years of experience and her own fitness studio. Ashley is a licensed nutritionist who has several years of experience working at her local hospital and clients in her community. Demi has a wide background in health science and fitness along with 7 years of personal training, and Michelle has a background in psychology and work experience in mental health and wellbeing. Together we have come up with the dream team, along with our workers in the background. We look forward to working with you in helping you achieve your health and wellbeing goals.
